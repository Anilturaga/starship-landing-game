Catch the Ship

Bring Ship down from 2.6 km and set it into the tower's arms before the propellant runs out.

How to fly
  • Glide: tilt the nose down to glide toward the tower, and up to slow down.
  • Flip: at about 1.2 km, light the Raptors with a little throttle and pull back to stand the Ship up.
  • Settle: throttle against the fall and nudge the stick to drift over the arms.
  • Cues: line up the white dot inside the blue ring in the scope above the stick. Above the throttle, ▲ means push up, ▼ pull down, ● hold.
  • Camera: drag the screen to look around, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset.
